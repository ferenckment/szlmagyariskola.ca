We are pleased to inform families about the new groups and programs starting this school year.

### Middle group – ages 8–10

A new middle group is starting for children aged 8–10.

- **Start:** October 18 (the week after Thanksgiving)
- **When:** Sundays, 9:30–11:00 a.m.
- **Where:** in the church

### Advanced group – ages 14–17

An advanced group is starting for students aged 14–17 who read and write Hungarian well. Its topic is Hungarian and European literature.

- **When:** every Monday at 4:00 p.m.
- **Start:** September 28
- **Where:** online

### Plans from January

We hope to start a kindergarten group in January as well.

We will let families know about further details and exact times well in advance.
